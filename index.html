Highlight of the day: Got caught in the rain

Author: Nhi

September 23, 2025
  1. Woke up in the morning, did not bring umbrella
  2. CSE 325 class at 10:20 AM, my tablet was not charged and was at 13 percent battery
  3. CSE 325 class ended at 11:40 AM, I gazed at the darkened sky and realized I am but a knight without his armor (raincoat) and sword (umbrella), my princess (bag with all my electronics in it) cried
  4. It rained so hard outside that a 10 minute walk is enough to give me a full shower I had to go back to change in fear of getting a cold

Other noteworthy event

I finally gave Sleep Token new album "Even in Arcadia" a listen,... and it is unfortunately not my biggest cup of tea. As someone who was drawn to the band by their heavier tracks, this is too far from their roots. Some might argue their development is similiar to that of Metallica after the AJFA album, but I digress for a number of reasons, which the top one is that Sleep Token pop to rock/metal ratio is getting concerning. For what its worth, I do like Look To Windward and Emergence. Now that I think about it, some of my favorites from them are not that heavy either, but for some reason I enjoy them better (Gods, Descending, The Summoning, The Apparition). I just pray they do not go EDM in the future.

Last note, my mouse also stopped working all of the sudden. I gave it fresh batteries and all but I think there is something wrong with the bluetooth and usb reciever inside the mouse, which at the moment I have no way to fix as I do not have the necessary equipment with me, so the only resort is to buy a new one. It's a shame because I have only had it for 5 years. I was considering between another Logitech mouse, or I can use something less expensive for the time being.
